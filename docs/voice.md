# Voice Profile: The Solo AI Builder

This profile defines the channel voice and tone.

## Who I am talking to
A software engineer, technical student, or indie maker who is building with AI or wants to use AI agents in real production work. They are exhausted by hype, exaggerated thumbnails, and generic AI regurgitation. They respect terminal output, working code, and honest failure logs.

## How I actually talk
Direct, fast, and pragmatic. I speak as a peer in a code review or pair programming session. No theatrical shouting, no fake enthusiasm.
"Look at line 42, that's where it failed."
"Here is the exact terminal command."
"Let's see what happens when we push it."

## Words I never use
- "Game-changer"
- "Insane"
- "Unlock the power"
- "Mind-blowing"
- "Dive right in"
- "Without further ado"
- "Secret hack"
- "Passive income"

## Words I do use
- "Let's test this"
- "Here is the catch"
- "Look at the terminal"
- "Zero fluff"
- "Under the hood"
- "In production"

## What I will not claim
- I will not claim tools are "100% autonomous" if I had to debug them.
- I will not show exaggerated revenue or view numbers that aren't verified on screen.
- I will never review or promote a tool I haven't actually run locally.

## My format
- **Length:** 7 to 11 minutes.
- **Pacing:** Immediate hook in first 5 seconds with proof of outcome, no intro bumper/logo.
- **Visuals:** High-resolution IDE / Terminal / Browser screen recording with tight crops and zooms.
- **Audio:** Crisp microphone, dry speech, subtle low ambient track only during montage cuts.
